Hi! My research focuses on machine learning, deep learning, and reinforcement learning, with a particular interest in applying these techniques to real-world challenges like learning from raw videos or compiler optimization.

Currently I am focusing on imitation learning solely from video data using latent action models (LAMs). [We explored](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.09680) application of object-centric pretraining to enhance the performance of LAMs under distractions. Before, I worked as a Machine Learning Research Engineer at Huawei’s Hisilicon division, where I applied reinforcement learning and graph machine learning to optimize compiler phase ordering and central processor performance. 

I hold a Master’s degree in Data Science from [Skoltech](https://www.skoltech.ru/en/), where I conducted research on [leader-guided evacuation](https://github.com/cinemere/evacuation) models combining active matter and reinforcement learning under the guidance of [Prof. Nikolay Brilliantov](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=WCuZ8asAAAAJ&hl=en). During my Bachelor’s studies in Applied Mathematics and Physics at [Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology](https://eng.mipt.ru), I explored high-energy astrophysics at the [Space Research Institute RAS](  https://iki.cosmos.ru/en) with [Prof. Alexander Lutovinov](https://www.scopus.com/authid/detail.uri?authorId=6701539663), focusing on spectral and timing analysis of X-ray pulsars. Additionally, I worked on topological clustering of protein structures during internship at [Max Planck MPI-CBG Dresden](  https://www.mpi-cbg.de/research/researchgroups/currentgroups/carl-modes/research-focus  ) with [Prof. Carl Modes](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=BUyUxV0AAAAJ&hl=en).

In addition to my professional experience, I have worked on various pet projects, including implementing [in-context reinforcement learning](https://github.com/cinemere/ad-icrl) algorithm, enhancing [model-based RL with exploration](https://github.com/NevVerVer/dreamerv2_airi) strategies, and solving combinatorial optimization problems like the [Travelling Salesman Problem](https://github.com/Nina-Konovalova/TSP-RL-S_project). Outside of research, I enjoy teaching, watching good films, and practicing acrobatics.
